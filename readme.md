# SONIC VANE

Sonic VANE (LS5 Engine 3) is a physics-sandbox-styled action platformer framework built on the Godot engine.  It is a sequel to LS5's final BlitzSonic mod, LS5 Engine 2 / Project Salute.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
This project is developed in the spirit of the late-2000s hobbyist game development community, specifically to spark that era of creativity and intrinsically-motivated learning.
You are encouraged to experiment, create forks, build your own projects, share your creations, and show them off, too.
Our goal is to encourage creativity, experimentation, and community participation, and we have no intention to hold control of the work of others.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Open Source Data and Package Transparency

This project is distributed under the **GNU General Public License, Version 3 (GPLv3)** and is developed with an emphasis on transparency, accessibility, education, modification, and community participation.

### Unencrypted Package Files

Official distributions of this project use **unencrypted package files**, located within the `Data` directory.

These packages are intentionally accessible, allowing users and developers to inspect, extract, study, modify, and repurpose their contents in accordance with the project's applicable licenses.

Encryption, deliberate obfuscation, and unnecessary restrictions on access to project data are discouraged.

### Inclusion of Project Data

Contributors and developers preparing distributions are encouraged to include **as much project data as reasonably possible** within the accessible package files.

Where practical:

- Game assets, configuration files, scripts, and other project resources should remain accessible and extractable.
- Data should be stored in documented, commonly understood formats where feasible.
- Files should not be deliberately encrypted or obfuscated for the purpose of preventing community inspection or modification.
- Resources necessary to understand, rebuild, or modify the project should be made available in their preferred editable forms, as required by GPLv3.
- Any excluded resources, proprietary dependencies, or technical limitations should be clearly documented.

### Community Modifications and Distributions

Community members are welcome to experiment with the project, create modifications, develop forks, and showcase their work.

Private experimentation, screenshots, videos, and demonstrations do not, by themselves, require publication of modified source code.

When distributing modified versions of GPL-covered software, contributors must comply with GPLv3, including its requirements concerning licensing notices and availability of Corresponding Source.

**Community members are encouraged to preserve the project's philosophy of accessible, inspectable, and modifiable data in their own distributions.**

### Our Philosophy

We believe that open-source development should encourage curiosity rather than discourage it.

The decision to distribute unencrypted package files is intentional, and is at the foundation of the game's asset-loading structure. We want people to explore how the software works, learn from its contents, experiment with modifications, and contribute their own ideas.
